# INT-02: National Agricultural Value Chain Development Project (NAVCDP)

## Status

Preliminary evidence assessment complete using public official reporting through November 2025. Causal impact is not assessable with public aggregate data.

## Intervention

NAVCDP (World Bank Project P176758) aims to increase market participation and value addition for targeted farmers in select value chains in project areas. Its components cover producer capacity, climate-smart value-chain ecosystem investments, urban food-system pilots, project coordination, and contingency response.

## Reported monitoring results

The December 2025 World Bank implementation report, based on the 2025 midterm review, reported:

- 1,329,542 direct beneficiaries reached after validation and deduplication.
- 620,530 reported female beneficiaries.
- 535,560 farmers adopting Technology, Innovation, and Management Practices.
- 43.23 for the market-sales indicator and 23.80 for value-added sales, both below end targets.
- 74.06% of revised financing disbursed.
- Zero vulnerable and marginalised groups supported through microprojects, against an end target of 2,000.

## Assessment conclusion

The public evidence supports a finding of substantial reported implementation and intermediate-result progress. It does not establish causal effects on yield, farm income, food security, resilience, sustainability, equity of outcomes, or value for money.

The public report notes that market measures are provisional midterm-review estimates and that the beneficiary total was revised after validation and deduplication. Beneficiary-level administrative data, expenditure records, survey microdata, technical documentation, and comparable non-beneficiary data are required for a causal assessment.
