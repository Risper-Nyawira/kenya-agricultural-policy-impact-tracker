# INT-01: National Value Chain Support Programme / Agricultural E-voucher

## Status

Preliminary evidence assessment complete. Causal impact is not assessable with public data currently available.

## Intervention

The National Value Chain Support Programme (NVSP) used an agricultural e-voucher mechanism to facilitate access to subsidised agricultural inputs. Public World Bank material describes a digital system intended to replace the manual input-subsidy process and deliver eligible farmers access to inputs through mobile phones and local retailers.

## Public evidence reviewed

- World Bank 2020 policy-financing documentation.
- World Bank results brief on Kenya's digital agricultural technologies.
- Public KNBS county maize production data for 2012-2020.
- Public KNBS monthly wholesale commodity-price data for 2012-September 2024.

## Preliminary findings

- Official reporting described 82,000 beneficiaries by March 2020, with about 60% reported female.
- Ministry-reported preliminary results referenced a 25% yield increase. This is retained as a reported programme claim, not a causal estimate.
- Public maize production and wholesale-price data provide national/county context but cannot identify NVSP beneficiaries or isolate the programme's effect from weather, price shocks, and other policies.

## Assessment conclusion

Implementation and reported reach can be documented. Productivity, livelihood, food-security, equity, efficiency, and causal impact conclusions require anonymised beneficiary, redemption, expenditure, location, and outcome data, ideally linked to a credible comparison group.

## Key source

World Bank, [Scaling Up Disruptive Technologies for Agricultural Productivity in Kenya](https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2023/06/07/scaling-up-disruptive-technologies-for-agricultural-productivity-in-kenya).
