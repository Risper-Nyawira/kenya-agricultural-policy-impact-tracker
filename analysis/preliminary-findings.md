# Preliminary Findings Across INT-01 and INT-02

## What can be concluded

Both interventions have sufficient public documentation to define the intervention, reconstruct programme logic, record official indicators, and describe reported implementation or monitoring progress.

INT-01 documents a digital input-support mechanism and reported beneficiary reach. INT-02 documents a broader agricultural value-chain programme with reported delivery, adoption, market, financial-inclusion, irrigation, and sustainable-land-management indicators.

## What cannot be concluded yet

Neither intervention has public evidence adequate for a credible causal impact estimate across the Tracker's five dimensions. Aggregate implementation reports cannot isolate programme effects from weather, market conditions, other policies, participant selection, or wider economic change.

Value for money is also not assessable from aggregate disbursement figures. It requires audited expenditure and clear unit-cost and result measures.

## Portfolio value

The Tracker demonstrates a transparent applied-research workflow: using public evidence carefully, documenting missing information, avoiding unsupported impact claims, and creating a concrete data-acquisition plan for stronger future evaluation.
